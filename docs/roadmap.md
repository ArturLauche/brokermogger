# Roadmap

The accepted workflow-completion scope is implemented: resumable onboarding,
editable profiles and identifiers, adult household consent, revisioned letters,
bound approval, fenced dispatch and recovery, manual submissions, confirmed
correspondence, reminders, escalation preparation, notifications, complete reports
and masked or explicitly confirmed sensitive exports. Encryption, immediate access
blocking and resumable erasure apply to the stored personal content.

Operators can curate broker methods and coverage, publish immutable legal/template
versions, adjust fair-use settings and inspect worker and audit health. Coverage
includes 30 EU/EEA countries and 20 current US state packs, with voluntary requests
where statutory support is absent. Separately sourced future laws remain inactive
or unpublished until their recorded commencement and required operator review.
The three sourced real broker drafts start disabled and unverified.

See [implementation and verification](implementation-progress.md),
[legal sources](legal-sources.md), [interface comparisons](interface-verification.md)
and [measured performance](performance.md). Production activation, independent legal
review and verifying a particular broker's methods remain instance-operator duties.

Possible later work, outside that completed scope:

- Additional independently reviewed country/state packs and broker methods.
- Verified public-holiday calendars with declared coverage and review dates.
- Broader measured PDF, storage and provider workloads before changing concurrency.
- Additional optional classification rules, always retaining user confirmation.

Billing, scraping, guaranteed removal, children's profiles and a mandatory
legal-reviewer queue remain out of scope.
